    Before choosing a plan

    Do I own the personality I subscribe to?

    No. In this fictional product, you license a style catalogue. PERSONA retains control of the core objectives, permitted wording, and profile updates—even on the highest individual tier.

    What can an organization control?

    The fictional Organization tier lets an administrator restrict profiles, require approval for switching, and set retention rules. In this exhibition, those permissions are displayed as a policy specification; no real employer or employee records exist.

    What happens when I stop paying?

    The fictional subscription reverts to Essential at the end of its billing period. Your basic speech decoding would remain separate in the imagined BCI world. This exhibit collects no money and does not operate a subscription.

    Does more expensive mean more authentic?

    That is the question. Paid access expands the approved catalogue. It does not establish that the selected voice is more truthful, healthier, or morally better.

    ABOUT THE ARTIFACT

    Who gets to author you?

    PERSONA imagines a 2050 market for communication personalities. A speculative technology that began with restoring speech now offers a catalogue of more socially desirable voices.

    Choose a situation and inspect seven exchanges in two versions: a reserved or blunt original, and a company-shaped personality. Each exchange contains one message from you and one reply from the other character. All conversations are prewritten fiction. No model, API, live participant or neural recording is involved.

    The policy code is illustrative pseudocode; it explains the fictional company's governance rather than running a personality model. The dialogue scripts enact those editorial choices. Numerical weights are invented policy targets, not measured traits or research findings.

    Research links refer to real work on humor, emotion regulation, empathy, politeness and structured communication. They do not validate purchasable personalities or demonstrate that language changes cause personality change.

    All interactions stay in this tab. Exports download to your device. The site uses no conversation database or real billing.
