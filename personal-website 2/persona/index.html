ABOUT THIS ARTIFACT

Who decides who you become?

PERSONA imagines a 2050 market for AI-assisted communication styles. A tool that began by restoring speech now offers a more empathetic, gentle, or confident version of your words.

You type a simulated decoded utterance. The left conversation receives it unchanged. The right receives an alternative expression. Jamie is an AI character in both; neither side is a real person.

The real research decoded attempted speech in a person with severe paralysis. It did not read private thoughts or demonstrate purchasable personality change. Those steps are our speculation.

Can learning from better exchanges be a form of growth? Can it also reward conformity? Would a subscription business want you to stop needing it?

Conversations are kept in this browser tab, not saved to a database. In live mode, messages are sent to the configured AI provider to generate replies. Simulated plans do not collect payment details.

Scientific starting point: Metzger et al., Nature (2023)
