Research

Research in HCI

Published / ICMI 2026 ACM · Napoli, Italy · October 5-9, 2026

EmotiStage: Designing an Emotion-Driven Performance System with Multi-modal Generative AI to Support Emotional Awareness

Ziying Wang*, Yihong Lin*, Xianglin Zhao, Yirui Huang, Ziya Zhou, Wei Xue, Wanling Cai, and Yucheng Jin

*Equal contribution; co-first authors.

Student Researcher · Duke Kunshan University

Hybrid Interactive Intelligence Lab · Mar-Sep 2025

Published in the Proceedings of the International Conference on Multimodal Interaction (ICMI 2026). EmotiStage supports emotional articulation and reflection through a creator-to-spectator theatrical performance workflow.

  • Connected GPT-4o-guided narratives, LeVo music generation, DALL-E 3 backdrops, and emotion-avatar performances in one multimodal experience.
  • Conducted a mixed-methods study with 13 participants using pre/post measures, emotion-word free listing, semi-structured interviews, and thematic analysis.
  • Found gains in emotional awareness and clarity (d = 1.53) and emotional granularity (d = 2.90), both p < .001; SUS was 81.35/100 and PETS was 78.37/100.

01 / Data Visualization

Lion and Car

A car dataset is translated into a grid of animated lions. Each lion represents one vehicle, with visual attributes mapped from MPG, horsepower, cylinders, weight, origin, and year.

  • Hover reveals car details near the cursor.
  • Click switches pages through the dataset.
  • Motion and blinking make the data feel more alive.

02 / Image Transformation

Kaleidoscope

An uploaded image becomes a kaleidoscopic field through tile extraction, symmetry, copying, and repeated image transformation.

  • Mouse buttons switch between original, tiled, and symmetric views.
  • Arrow keys adjust tile size and visual granularity.
  • The interaction recreates the shifting perspective of a childhood kaleidoscope.

03 / Emotional System

Bleeding Mood

A symbolic interactive pattern made from eyes, tears, and hearts. The work visualizes negative emotion as something wounded, visible, and still deserving protection.

  • Eyes form the hidden word BLOOD in the background.
  • Hover changes pupils into a calmer blue-black state.
  • Clicking hearts reveals a broken emotional state.

04 / Parametric Portrait

Portraiture

An interactive portrait generated directly from the original human photograph. Three rendering systems preserve the face while changing how color, texture, and movement reconstruct it.

  • Move the pointer to bend particles, redirect threads, and shift scanlines.
  • Click or press 1–3 to switch between three clearly different modes.
  • Each mode keeps the original face visible while rebuilding its texture and movement.
